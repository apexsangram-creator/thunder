Route 66 Gifts & Souvenirs

At Thunder-Rode, Route 66 isn’t just a highway, it’s a heartbeat. Our Route 66 Gifts & Souvenirs celebrate the spirit of the open road and the legends who ride it. Inside our Kingman, Arizona shop, you’ll find everything from collectible Route 66 signs, caps, and patches to locally made keepsakes that capture the grit and charm of America’s most iconic stretch of asphalt. Whether you’re a traveler chasing the Mother Road or a biker adding miles to your story, these souvenirs carry more than a logo, they carry the memory of your journey. Stop in, grab a piece of Route 66 history, and take a little of Thunder-Rode with you wherever the next ride leads.
